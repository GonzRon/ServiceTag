# ServiceTag 1.8.0

ServiceTag 1.8.0 (version code 21) is a feature release. ServiceTag now speaks nine more languages, the Maintenance tab gathers maintenance groups, supplies and installed components into one section, Reminder health moves to Settings and says plainly when nothing is wrong, and linking an asset to Home Assistant lets you choose the helper by its name. It installs in place over 1.7.0, and nothing about your data or your backups changes. ServiceTag 1.8.0 is the first release whose source is published under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPLv3); see [LICENSE](../../LICENSE).

## Languages (#102)

ServiceTag's words now come in Spanish, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, French, Japanese, Russian, German, Italian and Hindi, as well as English. ServiceTag follows the phone's language. On Android 13 and later you can also give ServiceTag a language of its own in Android's settings (Settings › System › Languages › App languages, or ServiceTag's app info page), which offers exactly these ten.

Dates, dates with times and decimal numbers are written the language's own way, and in a language that writes decimals with a comma, a number typed as "0,5" is read as a half. A date field shows and takes dates the way your language and region write them — "03/01/2026" in the US, "01.03.2026" in German — and its hint names that format; a date typed as 2026-03-01 is still read.

Only what ServiceTag says changes. What you typed — names, notes, serial and model numbers, units — is shown exactly as you entered it, and nothing of yours is translated. Backups, Transfer Packs, the Developer API, the MCP tools and NFC tags carry the same language-neutral values as before, so changing the phone's language changes nothing but what the screen says, and a backup made on a phone in one language restores on a phone in another. ServiceTag's notification channels in Android's settings take the language's names too.

## The Maintenance tab and Reminder health (#103)

The Maintenance tab now reads **Due work**, **Schedules**, then one section, **Groups, supplies and components**, with three rows: **Maintenance groups**, **Supplies** and **Installed components**. **Maintenance groups** opens the group list on a page of its own, where you create a group as before. **Installed components** lists everything fitted now, across all your assets, each with the asset it is fitted in; tap one to open that asset, where you edit, replace or remove it.

The tab's **Reminders** row is gone. **Reminder health** is now in Settings › Utilities, between **Categories** and **Home Assistant**, and the **REMINDER FAILED** badge on the Dashboard and on the Maintenance tab still opens it. Its checks, findings and repairs are unchanged. When the last check found nothing, the page no longer looks empty: it says "No problems found.", when that check ran ("Last checked … at …"), and, under **Checks that passed**, each of the seven checks, from "Notifications are allowed" to "Every meter schedule has a baseline reading".

## Choosing a Home Assistant entity (#105)

Linking an asset to Home Assistant no longer means knowing the helper's entity ID. On the **Link to Home Assistant** sheet, tap **Choose entity**: ServiceTag reads Home Assistant's on/off helpers (its `input_boolean` entities) and lists them by name, each over its entity ID. **Search by name or entity ID** narrows the list. Tap the helper that is on while this asset is in season, and the sheet shows it with **Change** beside it. **Save** waits until you have chosen a helper or typed an entity ID.

The list is read only when you tap **Choose entity** or **Refresh**, from the address you entered, with the same token and the same network rule as every other check, and nothing is written to Home Assistant. If the list cannot be read, the sheet says why, in the words a check uses, and keeps what you had chosen and the last list it read. **Enter entity ID manually** is always there, on the sheet and in the list, for an entity ID you would rather type. Either way the link stores the exact entity ID, never the name: a helper you rename in Home Assistant keeps working, and the next list shows its new name. Linking, the season and the checks that follow work exactly as in 1.7.0. The setup and the full limits are in [Home Assistant season sync](../home-assistant-season-sync.md).

## Fixes (#94, #99, #101)

- A schedule sent through the Developer API with a key and no value is refused as a bad request instead of failing as an error inside the app, and nothing is written (#94; see below).
- The test that proves a backup with nothing transferred out keeps every record now checks every list a backup carries; before, four of them could be empty and the test still passed (#99). Backups and Transfer Packs themselves are unchanged.
- The security and privacy documents now name both of ServiceTag's uses of the network, Save as document and the Home Assistant season sync, and [Local-first design, privacy, and Android permissions](../local-first-and-permissions.md) says when ServiceTag uses the network and why each permission is there (#101).

## Developer API & MCP

**One thing a script could meet:** `POST /v1/schedules` and `PATCH /v1/schedules/{id}` answer a body with a key and no value, such as `{"title":}`, with **400** `bad_request` carrying the parser's own message, where they answered **500** `internal`, and nothing is written (#94). Two keys with no comma between them were already a 400 on these two routes and still are.

Nothing else changed: no route, field, status or code was added or removed, there are still 28 sub-resources under `/v1/assets/{id}/`, and the MCP server is 1.7.0's, unchanged, with 90 tools. The API and the MCP carry the same language-neutral values whatever the phone's language. Choosing an entity happens on the phone only; `GET /v1/assets/{id}/season-sync` and the MCP tool `get_season_sync` read the same link as before.

## Upgrading

- 1.8.0 installs in place over 1.7.0, and your data stays.
- The database stays at schema 21 and backups at format 20: nothing moves at first launch, a 1.8.0 export restores into 1.7.0, and a 1.7.0 export into 1.8.0. Take an export before upgrading.
- On a phone set to Spanish, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, French, Japanese, Russian, German, Italian or Hindi, ServiceTag opens in that language after the update. To keep it in English on Android 13 or later, choose English for ServiceTag under Settings › System › Languages › App languages.
- Date fields show and take dates the way your language and region write them, no longer as 2026-03-01; a date typed that way is still read.
- The Maintenance tab's **Reminders** row is now **Reminder health** in Settings › Utilities.
- **Link to Home Assistant** starts with **Choose entity**; to type an entity ID as before, tap **Enter entity ID manually**. Assets already linked keep their links, and the connection and the token are untouched.

## Known limits

- The translations are drafted with the [localization glossary](../localization-glossary.md) and checked mechanically. They are not natively reviewed.
- When something fails, the technical reason inside a translated message can still be in English.
- The starter templates and built-in categories ServiceTag offers stay in English; once applied they are your own records, shown as written.
- Portuguese is written as in Brazil, for every Portuguese-speaking phone. A phone set to Traditional Chinese shows English, not Simplified Chinese.
- There is no language choice inside ServiceTag: Android's own per-app setting, on Android 13 and later, is the way to give it a language other than the phone's.
- After you change the language, a message already on the screen stays in the old language until the screen draws it again.
- Reminder health lists the checks that passed only when it found nothing; with a finding, it shows the findings and their repairs as before.
- Opening Reminder health runs its checks; "Last checked" is when that run finished, and it does not move while the page stays open.
- The **Installed components** list only lists and opens: you edit, replace or remove a component on its asset's page, and removed components are not listed there.
- **Choose entity** lists Home Assistant's `input_boolean` helpers only; any other entity ID is entered with **Enter entity ID manually**.
- Devices are not shown: the list neither groups helpers by device nor says which device one belongs to.
- The list is read when you tap **Choose entity** or **Refresh**, never in the background, so a helper added in Home Assistant meanwhile shows after **Refresh**. Past the first 2,000 helpers the list is cut and the sheet asks you to search; an answer over 8 MiB is refused ("Home Assistant sent an answer ServiceTag cannot read."), and manual entry remains.
- **Choose entity** is for linking an asset. It does not change the entity of an asset that is already linked.
- The system Back gesture while the list is open closes the whole sheet, and nothing is linked; the list's own **Cancel** returns to the sheet and keeps your choice.

The Developer API's contracts — every route, field and refusal named here — are in [`docs/api/v1.md`](../api/v1.md). How the language packs are built and kept complete is in [Localization](../localization.md).
