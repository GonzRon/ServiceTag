# ServiceTag 1.8.1

ServiceTag 1.8.1 (version code 22) is a fix release. A schedule that uses a form can now be completed from that form, the entry form has one button to save with, and Log maintenance on the Maintenance screen opens its list again. It installs in place over 1.8.0, and nothing about your data or your backups changes.

## Completing a schedule with its form (#117)

On 1.8.0 a schedule set to use a form could not be completed in the app. You opened the form from the schedule, filled it in and tapped **Record entry**. The entry was saved, but not as the schedule's service. The schedule stayed overdue, its page still said "No service recorded yet", and doing it again saved a second, identical entry.

Now the form completes the schedule.

- The entry is linked to the schedule and to the occurrence it completes.
- The due date moves on to the next occurrence.
- The schedule's standing reminder clears at once.
- It works from all four places a form can be opened from: the schedule's page, the **Log maintenance** list on the Maintenance screen, the scan sheet, and **Done** on a reminder.
- **Done** on a reminder opens the form once. Come back from the form, by saving or by closing it, and ServiceTag does not ask again.
- On the scan sheet, once one form is done the sheet goes on to the next form in its list.
- If a required reading is empty, the form says so and nothing is written. If the schedule was archived or deleted while the form was open, the form says it could not save, and nothing is written.
- A form with no profile starts with the schedule's title in its **Entry** field. You can change it, and what you type is kept.

A schedule that is set to Quick is completed as before. So are entries not opened from a schedule.

## Log maintenance on the Maintenance screen (#120)

Tapping **Log maintenance** on the Maintenance screen showed nothing. The list of due schedules closed before it was drawn, even when schedules were due. It had been so since 1.2.0. The list is read first now, and then it opens. When nothing is due, it still closes quietly, as before.

## One button on the entry form (#118)

The entry form had two controls that did the same thing: **Save** in the app bar, and **Record entry** at the foot of the form. Two names for one action read as two actions. The app bar's **Save** is gone, and the app bar keeps only the close button. The one button at the foot is **Record entry** on a new entry and **Save entry** when you edit one. Nothing else about the form changed.

## Developer API & MCP

Nothing changed. No route, field, status or code was added or removed, there are still 28 sub-resources under `/v1/assets/{id}/`, and the MCP server is 1.7.0's, unchanged, with 90 tools. The API's own completion route, `POST /v1/schedules/{id}/complete`, never had the form problem.

## Upgrading

- 1.8.1 installs in place over 1.8.0, and your data stays.
- The database stays at schema 21 and backups at format 20: nothing moves at first launch, a 1.8.1 export restores into 1.8.0, and a 1.8.0 export into 1.8.1. Take an export before upgrading.
- The Home Assistant connection and its token are untouched.
- A schedule that stayed overdue on 1.8.0 is still overdue after the update. Complete it from its form, and it moves on.

## Known limits

- With the keyboard open, **Record entry** can sit under the keyboard. Press Back once to close the keyboard, and the button is there. ServiceTag 1.8.2 (#121) fixes this.
- Entries that 1.8.0 saved from a schedule's form were not linked to the schedule, and they stay unlinked. Editing an entry cannot link it. If you recorded the same service more than once, delete the duplicates by hand, then complete the schedule from its form once.
- **Record entry** on a form opened from a schedule always completes that schedule's current occurrence. To log a reading that is not a service, open the form from the asset instead.
- A form schedule that also counts a meter takes the meter reading only if its form carries that reading, as in 1.8.0.
- One narrow case. **Done** on a reminder that asks for a meter reading records the service as soon as you answer, and may then ask a follow-up question. If Android ends ServiceTag before that question is answered, reopening ServiceTag can ask for the reading again, and answering it records the service a second time. Cancel it if it asks again.

The Developer API's contracts are in [`docs/api/v1.md`](../api/v1.md).
